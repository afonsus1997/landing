About

Afonso is an electronics engineer based in Lisbon. He holds an MSc in Electronics Engineering from Instituto Superior Técnico, where his thesis covered FPGA-based radio communications for CubeSats. His work spans mixed-signal hardware design, FPGA development and embedded software.

As a researcher with the IST Nanosatlab, he led the electronics work on ISTSAT-1, from the ground support equipment to the qualification and launch campaigns. The satellite launched on the maiden flight of Ariane 6.

Outside of work he designs open-source hardware, including FPGA development boards and electronic badges. He is a licensed radio amateur with an interest in satellite tracking, and also enjoys astrophotography, birdwatching, 3D printing and self-hosting. He studies music theory and plays jazz piano.
